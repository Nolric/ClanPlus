The biggest line is also the least visible. That's why it's explained just below, rather than buried in a total.

Why I spell it out

Plenty of projects ask for support without saying what it pays for. The third line is the largest and the least obvious: these aren't servers, they're coding assistance tools. Without them Clan Plus would move several times slower: the strategy editor, the replay debrief and the SR probably wouldn't exist yet.

You may well decide that isn't what a donation is for, and that's an entirely fair position. It's precisely so you can judge for yourself that I'm writing it down.

02

What it changes

In concrete terms, what the money goes to.

STORAGE

Replays stay

Each battle weighs a few hundred kilobytes. That's what lets you replay a game six months later, and it's the line that keeps growing.

DEVELOPMENT

The work carries on

A heatmap of where players die, plan versus execution, synergy analysis: all of it is planned.

NEVER

No advertising

This is a tool you use during a clan session, not a blog. A banner has no place in it.

The commitment

No feature will ever become paid. Not the editor, not the replays, not the SR, not the stats. If the funding doesn't follow, I'll cut costs: I won't put a toll gate on what's already there.
