Measure

A rating that measures teamplay.

The Synergy Rating weighs your real share of the win against how strong the opposition was — not raw damage. And it shows its margin of error.

Measured, not claimed: over 604 tier X battles, the season 1 SR picks the winner 68 times out of 100 (± 5, cross-validated).

The mod

It all starts with one file.

The Clan Plus mod records every clan battle — positions second by second, shots, damage and who dealt it — and sends it to the site the moment the fight ends. Nothing shows during the battle.

In the garage, its window shows your slot for tonight and checks every tank: setup, ammo, crew, a clan lock still running.

Rules of engagement.

  • Free, for good. No paid feature, ever. If funding falls short, costs get cut — nothing gets a paywall.
  • No ads. It's a tool you use during a clan session, not a blog.
  • Your password stays with Wargaming. Sign-in goes through the official Wargaming site.
  • Margins of error, shown. When there isn't enough data, the site says so instead of guessing.
  • Independent. A community project, not affiliated with Wargaming.
